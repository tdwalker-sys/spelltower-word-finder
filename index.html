Type the board instead

One row per line, top row first. . empty, # black tile, digit after a letter for a minimum-length tile (E6), * after a letter for a blue row-clear tile (Z*), ! after a letter for a yellow bonus tile (R!), @ after a letter for Search mode's star tile (P@).

Scores are estimates: letter values of every tile the word removes, times word length, times 2 with one yellow bonus tile in the word (★), 3 with two, and so on. Words of 5+ letters also remove the tiles directly above, below, left and right of each letter (not the diagonals). J, Q, X, Z and blue tiles clear their whole row, black tiles included. Orange outline marks the tiles that would be removed. In Search mode, mark the starred tile (the reader does this, or tick "★ star" on the tile) and only words that pass through it are listed; tiles there do not disappear, so do not tap Play. An orange ? is a tile the screenshot reader could not read; tap it to fix.
