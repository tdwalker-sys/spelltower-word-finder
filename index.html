One row per line, top row first. . empty, # black tile, digit after a letter for a minimum-length tile (E6), * after a letter for a blue row-clear tile (Z*), ! after a letter for a yellow bonus tile (R!), @ after a letter for Search mode's star tile (P@), ^ after a letter for a tile in a bubble (T^).

Scores are estimates: letter values of every tile the word removes, times word length, times 2 with one yellow bonus tile in the word (×2 on the word), 3 with two (×3), and so on. Words of 5+ letters also remove the tiles directly above, below, left and right of each letter (not the diagonals). J, Q, X, Z and blue tiles clear their whole row, black tiles included. Orange outline marks the tiles that would be removed. In Search mode, mark the starred tile (the reader does this, or tick "★ star" on the tile). Words through it are the scoring words and come first; playing one ends the game, so it is the last move. Words that miss it are listed after them as setup words, with no score: they are played first, to open the board for a better ★ word. Tap a setup word to see the best ★ word the board would offer after that tracing, then Play it to follow along. A setup word that would remove the ★ tile without using it (as a 5+ letter word's neighbour, or in a row clear) is listed last and tagged "removes ★"; what the game does then has not been checked. The ★ stays on its letter as tiles drop. An orange ? is a tile the screenshot reader could not read; tap it to fix. "Words through this tile" (in the tile editor) keeps only words with a tracing through that tile; tap more tiles and press it again to add them, and choose whether a word must pass through every selected tile or any one of them. In Tower, select the tiles you want gone. In Search, select the ★ tile and the yellow tiles you want to reach. With "Spare blue tiles" ticked, each word is shown with a tracing that removes no blue tile where one exists, and words that cannot avoid removing one (in the word, beside a 5+ letter word, or in a row another blue tile clears) are listed last under their own heading; −1 blue on a word is the number of blue tiles its tracing removes. Bubble Puzzle: a letter in a bubble cannot be used in a word. Any word pops a bubble that shares an edge with one of its letters (not a corner); the letter stays and can be used from then on. A word of 5+ letters pops a bubbled neighbour rather than removing it. With "Bubble rules" ticked (the reader ticks it when it finds a bubble), a blue tile in the word pops the bubbles in its row instead of clearing the row. pops 2 on a word is the number of bubbles it pops; a dashed ring on the board marks them. Popping adds nothing to the score estimate.
